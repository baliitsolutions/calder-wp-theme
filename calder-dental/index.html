What happens at your first visit

About 45 minutes, start to finish. Nothing is done without your say-so.

  1. A quick chat

    Tell us what's bothering you, what you'd like to change, and anything that makes you nervous.

  2. Exam and photos

    We check teeth and gums and show you the photos on screen, so you see what we see.

  3. Your plan, in writing

    You leave with options, timings and fixed prices. Take it home and decide in your own time.

Questions

Do you take emergency patients?

Yes. Call before 10am and we'll see you the same day whenever we can, including new patients.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Tell us when you book. We'll give you a longer slot, explain each step before we do it, and agree a stop signal. Sedation is available for longer treatments.

Which insurance do you accept?

We work with most major dental insurers and can claim on your behalf. Bring your card to your first visit.

Is there parking?

Free parking for patients behind the building, with step-free access to every treatment room.
